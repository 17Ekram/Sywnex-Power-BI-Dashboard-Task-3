📊 E-Commerce Sales & Performance Dashboard | Power BI

I’m excited to share my E-Commerce Sales & Performance Dashboard, developed as part of my Data Analyst Internship at Swynex Technologies.

The objective of this project was to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful KPIs, trends, comparisons, and business insights using Python/Pandas and Power BI/DAX.

🔎 Key Business Insights

 💰 Total Sales: ₹23.20M

 💵 Total Profit: ₹7.30M

 📦 Total Quantity Sold: 19.14K

 🛒 Total Orders: 10K

 👥 Unique Customers: 4,336

 📈 2025 Sales: ₹11.72M vs ₹11.48M in 2024, representing approximately 2.09% growth

 🥇 Electronics was the highest-selling category with ₹4.66M, contributing about 20.1% of total sales.

 🌐 Website was the leading sales channel with ₹9.06M, approximately 39% of total sales.

 💳 UPI was the most frequently used payment method with 2,979 transactions.

 🗺️ West recorded the highest regional sales at approximately ₹5.86M.

 👨‍💼👩‍💼 Sales were almost evenly distributed between male and female customers.

 📦 Delivered orders accounted for approximately 59% of sales by order status.

 💰 Average Sales per Customer: approximately ₹5.35K.

 🛍️ Average Order Value: approximately ₹2.32K.

🛠️ Functions & Techniques Used.



1. Used Chat Gpt to create a Ecommerce Dataset of 10000 rows and 23 columns.

2. Used Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib and Plotly to Data Cleaning, Preparation, Statistics, Visualization and Save Clean Dataset into csv file in MySql.

3. Used Power BI Dax Function like Sum(), Distinctcount(), Divide(), Calculate(), Dateadd().

Find KPIs : Total Sales, Total Cost, Total Profit, Total Quantity, Total Orders, Average Sales    per Customer, Average Order Value, Average Orders per Day, Average Sales per Day, Year-over-Year, Sales Growth % and KPIs to Created Attractive Dashbords
